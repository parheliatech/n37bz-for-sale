The Story

I bought this VariEze back in 2013 and spent a couple of years bringing it back to flying condition, working under the guidance of an A&P/IA the whole way through.

Since then I've logged several hundred happy hours in it, bombing around the country solo — it's a genuinely fun, efficient, and honest little airplane to fly. My situation has changed, though: I'm no longer a single guy, and the VariEze has been spending a lot more time in the hangar than in the air. It deserves an owner who will actually fly it.

We just finished a condition inspection, and it's time to let her go to a good home. Everything below is described as accurately as I can — ask questions, I'll answer them straight. If it helps, I'd also be happy to fly her to the new owner.
